AI didn't make you capable. It showed you what you are capable of.

The real question was never whether you can use it. It's whether what you're making with it is actually worth something, in service of what you're building, not just hype consuming your tokens.

Most people use AI like a vending machine. Put in a prompt, get out an answer, move on. That's not wrong. It's just small. The bigger use is treating it like an amplifier for what you're actually trying to build, your work, your brand, your next move, not a shortcut for busywork you didn't want to do anyway. Used that way, AI doesn't replace your worth. It proves it. It keeps showing you what you're capable of, over and over, in ways you didn't expect.

That happens in the messy middle. Not the strategy deck that says AI matters. Not a two-hour tutorial on prompting. The middle, where you're figuring out what you actually want, trying things, getting some of it wrong, until you find the shape of what you're capable of.

You don't need a technical background to get here. Most people who are ahead right now got there sideways, through curiosity, through building small things tied to something they actually cared about. The tool isn't the difference. The aim is.

About

I'm Alison Nicole Lee.

A year ago I didn't think of myself as someone who builds things. I was good at my job, good at giving other people advice, and quietly convinced that building was for other people.

Then I vibe-coded an app, just to see what would happen, and ended up with something I actually loved using. Somewhere in there I realized I was doing things I genuinely didn't think I was capable of. Not because the AI was doing them for me. Because it kept handing me back proof, over and over, that the capability had been there the whole time. I just hadn't had a reason to use it yet.

This isn't a tutorial account. I'm not going to teach you ten prompts. I'm going to show you what it actually looks like to use this stuff in service of something you care about enough to keep going once it gets messy. That's the real unlock. Not the tool. What you're willing to build with it.
